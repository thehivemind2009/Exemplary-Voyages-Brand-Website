08 Dining and retreats beneath the waves
01
The Muraka by Conrad Maldives

The world's first underwater hotel suite, where the master bedroom sits completely beneath the ocean surface surrounded by living coral. A two level private residence combining above water living with an immersive underwater chamber. Nothing in the Maldives compares to a night here.

Book Now
02
SEA by Anantara Kihavah

An all glass underwater restaurant in Baa Atoll where every table faces the reef directly. A curated tasting menu unfolds as manta rays and reef sharks pass by the windows at depth. One of the Maldives' most distinctive and sought after dining addresses.

Book Now
03
Ithaa by Conrad Maldives

The world's first undersea restaurant, set five metres below the lagoon surface inside a curved acrylic shell with 180-degree reef views. A set tasting menu unfolds above a living coral garden that glows as light filters down through the water. The original, and still the benchmark.

Book Now
04
Underwater Spa by Huvafen Fushi

The world's first underwater spa, with treatment rooms set beneath the lagoon surface surrounded by living coral. Treatments proceed in complete quiet as the reef passes by through curved glass panels. An encounter unlike anything available above the waterline.

Book Now
05
Subsix by Niyama Private Islands

An underwater restaurant and club reached by boat and descent, sitting six metres below the lagoon surface. By day a refined dining venue with reef views; by evening the only underwater nightclub in the Maldives. A genuinely one of a kind setting with nothing close to it elsewhere.

Book Now
06
M6m by Ozen Life Maadhu

An intimate private dining experience at six metres depth on the reef, designed exclusively for two. A bespoke menu, uninterrupted coral reef views, and complete seclusion below the surface. The most personal underwater dining the Maldives has to offer.

Book Now
07
H2O by You & Me Maldives

An underwater restaurant at this adults only resort, with tables set directly above the reef floor and surrounded by living coral formations. A curated menu designed around the setting, in an atmosphere that is quiet, close, and entirely unlike dining at the surface.

Book Now
08
Only Blu by Oblu Select Lobigili

A semi submerged dining experience at Oblu's boutique North Male Atoll resort, with direct reef views and a focused menu curated for the occasion. One of the more accessible underwater dining experiences in the Maldives, without any compromise on atmosphere or setting.

Book Now
