Cookie Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026  —  Exemplary Voyages (Pvt) Ltd

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how Exemplary Voyages uses them, and how you can control your cookie preferences. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve performance, and provide information to website owners.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies — Always Active

These enable core website functions such as page navigation, security and your cookie consent preference. Without these, the website cannot function properly.

Analytics Cookies — With Your Consent

These help us understand how visitors use our website. We use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation enabled. Data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. This helps us improve content and the user experience.

Marketing Cookies — With Your Consent

These allow us to show relevant content on partner platforms such as Meta (Instagram, Facebook) and Google. These cookies track visits across websites to deliver personalised advertising based on your interests. We do not share identifiable personal data with advertisers.

3. Managing Your Preferences

When you first visit our website, a consent banner allows you to accept all cookies, accept essential cookies only, or customise your preferences. You can update your choices at any time by clicking "Cookie Policy" in the footer of any page.

You may also control cookies directly through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. For more information, visit www.aboutcookies.org or your browser's help documentation.

4. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to withdraw your cookie consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal. To do so, click "Cookie Policy" in the footer and update your preferences.

5. Contact

For questions about our use of cookies, contact us at inquiries@exemplaryvoyages.com.
