Day One

Arrival & Negombo

20 min from Airport

Your ornithological journey begins the moment you arrive. A private transfer from the airport carries you to Negombo, where the coastal atmosphere offers a first taste of Sri Lanka's birdlife — the fishing birds over the Dutch Canal, the swifts over the lagoon. This evening, meet your expert birder to discuss the extraordinary avian encounters that await over the next two weeks.

Tonight you sleep at
Jetwing Beach
Negombo, Western Coast
Anawilundawa & Wilpattu
02
Day Two

Anawilundawa & Wilpattu

3.5 hours' drive

Dawn at the Anawilundawa Bird Sanctuary — a RAMSAR Wetland site where three ecosystems meet: coast, mangrove and freshwater tanks. The result is a concentrated mosaic of bird habitats found nowhere else in Sri Lanka. Rare species including the Fishing Cat and extraordinary waterbird assemblages make this one of the finest morning birding sites on the island.

Continue to Wilpattu National Park by early afternoon — one of Sri Lanka's oldest protected areas, its forest stillness in dramatic contrast to the wetland morning. An afternoon drive through the villus (natural lakes) offers sightings of painted stork, Asian paradise flycatcher and a remarkable range of forest birds.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Wilpattu Tree House
Wilpattu, North Western Province
Wilpattu to Sigiriya
03
Day Three

Wilpattu to Sigiriya

2.5 hours' drive

A full morning safari in Wilpattu — the park's dry lowland forest comes alive in the early hours. The painted stork, grey heron, crested serpent eagle and the elusive Asian paradise flycatcher are among the regularly recorded species. By midday, drive south-east to Sigiriya, where the ancient rock fortress rises above its surrounding bird sanctuary. Bird watch around the moats and garden tanks in the late afternoon — raptors, including Shaheen Falcon, are regularly seen soaring above.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Sigiriya Kings Resort
Sigiriya, Cultural Triangle
Kandy: Udawatta Kele
04
Day Four

Kandy: Udawatta Kele

2.5 hours' drive

Drive to Kandy and begin the morning with a guided walk through the Udawatta Kele Forest Sanctuary — a dense urban forest above the city that shelters kingfishers, flycatchers, Sri Lanka grey hornbill and the extraordinary Malabar trogon. The afternoon takes you to the cultural heart of Kandy: the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, the lake, the old quarter.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Radha Hotel
Kandy, Central Province
Nuwara Eliya: Victoria Park
05
Day Five

Nuwara Eliya: Victoria Park

2.5 hours' drive

The road climbs into the tea hills and arrives at Nuwara Eliya, Sri Lanka's highest town and one of the finest birding destinations in South Asia. This evening, Victoria Park — a colonial-era garden at the centre of town — is a notable winter birding site where the Indian Blue Robin, Kashmir Flycatcher and Pied Thrush make seasonal appearances during the northern winter months.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Araliya Green Hills
Nuwara Eliya, Hill Country
Horton Plains: Cloud Forest Walk
06
Day Six

Horton Plains: Cloud Forest Walk

At dawn, drive to Horton Plains National Park — a UNESCO-listed plateau of grassland and cloud forest at 2,100 metres. Walk the famous trail to World's End and Baker's Falls through a landscape that harbours species found nowhere else on earth: the Sri Lanka Whistling Thrush, Yellow-eared Bulbul, Dull-blue Flycatcher and the magnificent Horton Plains Bush Warbler. The afternoon continues at Hakgala Botanical Gardens, whose flowering trees attract an array of sunbirds and barbets.

Highlight

Arrive at Horton Plains before 7am for the best birding — the plateau mist lifts by mid-morning, and the most active species are concentrated near the forest edges in the early hours.

Tonight you sleep at
Araliya Green Hills
Nuwara Eliya, Hill Country
Udawalawe: Elephants & Birds
07
Day Seven

Udawalawe: Elephants & Birds

4 hours' drive

Drive south to Udawalawe National Park — famous above all for its large herds of wild elephant, but also an exceptional birding destination. Morning at Galways Land National Park for high-altitude specialists before the road descends through Ratnapura and into the dry zone. A late afternoon drive in Udawalawe offers extraordinary photography of elephants, peacock, grey heron and the vibrant Indian roller.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Grand Udawalawe Hotel
Udawalawe, Southern Province
Udawalawe to Tissamaharama
08
Day Eight

Udawalawe to Tissamaharama

1.5 hours' drive

A full morning safari in Udawalawe — the park's open grassland and reservoir edges teem with birds in the early hours. Majestic elephants move through the long grass; grey herons, painted storks, kingfishers and the vibrant peacock are regular sightings. By midday, continue to Tissamaharama, where the evening offers relaxed birding around the tank near your hotel.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Flameback Eco Resort
Tissamaharama, Southern Province
Weerawila & Debarawewa Lakes
09
Day Nine

Weerawila & Debarawewa Lakes

Two extraordinary lake systems wait this morning. Weerawila, with its vast shallow waters, attracts over 400 bird species throughout the year — lesser flamingo, spoonbill, painted stork, purple heron and the rare black-necked stork have all been recorded here. Debarawewa adds a contrasting waterside landscape in the afternoon, with excellent opportunities for close observation of water birds, otters and the superb sunbird.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Flameback Eco Resort
Tissamaharama, Southern Province
Yala: Leopards & Birding
10
Day Ten

Yala: Leopards & Birding

Morning and afternoon game drives in Yala National Park, where the birding complements the celebrated wildlife sightings. Crested hawk-eagle, brown fish owl, Indian pitta and the endemic Sri Lanka junglefowl are Yala residents; the possibility of leopard, elephant and sloth bear adds a thrilling dimension to every drive through the park's varied terrain of scrub forest, lagoon and open grassland.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Flameback Eco Resort
Tissamaharama, Southern Province
Bundala & Sinharaja
11
Day Eleven

Bundala & Sinharaja

2.5 hours' drive

Bundala National Park, a crucial RAMSAR Wetland, serves as a wintering ground for migratory waterbirds from as far as Siberia. Greater flamingo, Eurasian spoonbill, spot-billed pelican and rarities from the northern hemisphere are the highlight birds here in winter months. By afternoon, enter the Sinharaja Rainforest — the island's last primary lowland rainforest and a UNESCO World Heritage Site — where a first evening walk reveals the forest's unique character.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Jansen's Bungalow
Sinharaja, Sabaragamuwa
Sinharaja: The Rainforest
12
Day Twelve

Sinharaja: The Rainforest

A full day in Sinharaja — one of the most biodiverse places in Asia. The forest's endemic bird species are found nowhere else on earth: Sri Lanka Blue Magpie, Green-billed Coucal, Red-faced Malkoha, Ashy-headed Laughingthrush. Birds here travel in mixed-species feeding flocks that move through the canopy in a wave of colour and activity. Your ornithologist knows where they go. A jeep tour through the surrounding villages in the afternoon adds a cultural dimension to the day.

Highlight

Sinharaja's mixed-species bird flocks — sometimes comprising 20 or more species moving together through the forest — are one of the great wildlife spectacles in Asia. Your expert guide knows their circuits.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Jansen's Bungalow
Sinharaja, Sabaragamuwa
Sinharaja to Colombo
13
Day Thirteen

Sinharaja to Colombo

3 hours' drive

A final morning walk through the rainforest before the drive to Colombo — Sri Lanka's vibrant, layered capital. Arrive in the afternoon with time to explore: the Fort district, Pettah bazaar, the Galle Face Green. The city's Diyasaru Uyana wetland offers a final birding opportunity this evening — water birds and small mammals in a rewarding urban sanctuary.

Colombo: Final Day
14
Day Fourteen

Colombo: Final Day

A final morning at Diyasaru Uyana wetland before the afternoon is yours for last-minute exploration of the city. The National Museum, the Colombo Fort clock tower, the boutique galleries of the Cinnamon Gardens district, or simply a long lunch at one of Colombo's celebrated restaurants. Sri Lanka has given you its birds. Tonight, reflect on what you have witnessed.

15

Colombo to Airport: A Birder's Departure

After a final birding session at Diyasaru Uyana, your private transfer departs for Bandaranaike International Airport. Fifteen days of avian discovery across every landscape Sri Lanka possesses — from the mangrove wetlands of the northwest to the cloud forests of Horton Plains and the ancient canopy of Sinharaja. The birds of Sri Lanka will remain with you long after you leave.
