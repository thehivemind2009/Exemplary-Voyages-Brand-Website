Kotugoda: Arrival

30 min from Airport

Your private transfer from the airport carries you north to a quiet retreat on the outskirts of Negombo. This first evening is for settling in, for the first taste of the tropical air and the rhythm of Sri Lanka. Tomorrow, the adventure begins in earnest.

The Cultural Triangle
02
Day Two

The Cultural Triangle

3.5 hours' drive

Drive north into the heart of Sri Lanka's ancient civilisation. The Cultural Triangle encompasses three UNESCO World Heritage Sites within a few hours' drive — and your first day here is spent reaching Sigiriya via Dambulla Cave Temple. Stop at this extraordinary rock fortress of caves, whose interior walls carry over 1,500 square metres of ancient fresco work. Then press on to Sigiriya itself, arriving in time to watch the light change over the rock.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Aliya Resort
Sigiriya, Cultural Triangle
Sigiriya & Polonnaruwa
03
Day Three

Sigiriya & Polonnaruwa

Begin before dawn for the climb up Sigiriya Rock — Sri Lanka's most iconic sight and a UNESCO monument of world significance. The Lion Gate, the frescoed galleries, the mirror wall inscribed with verses by medieval visitors, and the summit plateau with its palace ruins and boundless views: Sigiriya rewards the early riser with extraordinary solitude.

By afternoon, drive to Polonnaruwa — Sri Lanka's medieval capital. Explore the ruins by bicycle: royal palaces, immense dagobas, the moonstone gateways and the magnificent Gal Vihara rock carvings.

Aliya Resort

Dambulla & Onwards
04
Day Four

Dambulla & Onwards

2.5 hours' drive

A morning in the Cultural Triangle — perhaps Minneriya National Park for elephant gathering, or a village spice garden — before heading south towards Kandy. The road descends from the dry plains into increasingly lush hills, the landscape transforming around you. Arrive in Kandy by late afternoon.

Tonight you sleep at

Kandy: Sacred & Beautiful
05
Day Five

Kandy: Sacred & Beautiful

A full day in the last Kandyan kingdom. Morning at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, then the Kandy market, the National Museum, and a walk around the lake. Afternoon: the Three Temple Loop by tuk tuk through the villages surrounding the city — a quiet journey through working Sri Lanka, ending at a spice plantation with highland views.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Cinnamon Citadel Kandy
Kandy, Central Province
Kandy to Hatton
06
Day Six

Kandy to Hatton

2.5 hours' drive

The road west from Kandy climbs into the tea country. You are heading for Hatton — a district of high-altitude estates, colonial bungalows and one of the island's best-kept secrets: a viewpoint known only to a handful of guides, accessible by a short walk through tea, where the landscape opens to reveal an extraordinary panorama of the central highlands.

Arrive at your estate bungalow and let the silence of the hills settle around you.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Ceylon Tea Trails
Hatton, Hill Country
The Secret Viewpoint
07
Day Seven

The Secret Viewpoint

Your guide takes you on foot this morning — through estate paths and forest tracks to the viewpoint that makes Hatton unique. The walk itself is the destination: past tea pluckers, through avenues of silver oak, emerging finally to a vantage point where Sri Lanka's mountains stack up in every direction. Return for a long lunch and an afternoon at leisure in the estate.

Highlight

An early morning walk to the secret viewpoint at first light offers the most ethereal experience, when the valleys below are still filled with mist and the peaks are perfectly clear above them.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Ceylon Tea Trails

Yala: Into the Wild
08
Day Eight

Yala: Into the Wild

4.5 hours' drive

Drive south from the hills to Sri Lanka's most celebrated national park. Yala is home to one of the world's highest densities of leopard, alongside elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles and hundreds of bird species. Arrive in time for an afternoon game drive — the light in the late afternoon is extraordinary, the dry forest alive with movement.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Wild Coast Tented Lodge
Yala National Park
Yala: Morning Safari
09
Day Nine

Yala: Morning Safari

The best time in Yala is the early morning, when the air is cool and the animals are active. Your guide takes you through the park's varied terrain — open scrubland, forest, lagoons, beach — tracking leopards by pug marks and reading the behaviour of the deer and peacocks. Return to camp for breakfast after the drive, then an afternoon at leisure in the lodge.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Wild Coast Tented Lodge
Yala National Park
Galle: The Fort
10
Day Ten

Galle: The Fort

3.5 hours' drive

The coast road west from Yala passes through Hambantota, Tangalle and Weligama before arriving at the great Dutch fort of Galle — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the finest intact colonial settlements in Asia. Spend the afternoon exploring the ramparts, the Dutch churches, the coral stone streets and the excellent restaurants within the fort walls.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Fort Bazaar
Galle Fort, Southern Province
Galle: The Southern Coast
11
Day Eleven

Galle: The Southern Coast

A final morning on the southern coast. The sea turtle hatcheries of Habaraduwa, the stilt fishermen at Koggala, the mangrove lagoons at Balapitiya — or simply a morning on the terrace of the fort hotel, watching the Indian Ocean. Afternoon is yours for last-minute exploration and a long dinner at one of Galle's celebrated tables.

◈
Tonight you sleep at
Fort Bazaar
Galle Fort, Southern Province
12

Galle to Airport: The Expressway North

After a final breakfast in Galle Fort, your private transfer joins the southern expressway — the fastest road in Sri Lanka — travelling north to Bandaranaike International Airport in time for your departure. Twelve days of discovery behind you; the stories will take longer still to fully arrive.
