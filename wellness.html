Exemplary Voyages — Wellness Philosophy
Destination One

Sri Lanka

Ancient Ayurvedic traditions, cloud forest stillness, and ocean air. Sri Lanka offers the depth that meaningful wellness requires.

Focused Group Wellness
Santani Wellness Resort, Kandy

Perched above a valley in the Knuckles Mountain Range, Santani is Sri Lanka's most awarded wellness destination. Designed for deep transformation, it offers medically guided Ayurveda, mindfulness and nutrition programmes. For small focused groups seeking a shared reset, Santani delivers rare privacy and intentional design in equal measure.

MICE Wellness
Shangri La Hambantota, Sri Lanka

Where world class conference infrastructure meets a 3,000-acre wildlife sanctuary. The Shangri La Hambantota offers sophisticated MICE facilities alongside a spa, ocean facing yoga pavilions and nature programming. Ideal for organisations that want their people to leave sharper, calmer and reconnected to purpose.

Destination Two

Maldives

Isolation by design. The Indian Ocean's silence is itself a treatment. The Maldives offers wellness without distraction.

Focused Group Wellness
Joali Being, Maldives

The Maldives' first dedicated wellness island. Joali Being is built around four pillars — mind, microbiome, energy and beauty — offering transformative programmes drawn from global healing traditions. For a group seeking genuine immersion rather than spa amenities, there is nowhere like it in the Indian Ocean.

MICE Wellness
Kagi Maldives Spa Island

Kagi is purpose built for those who have earned total sanctuary. An all inclusive wellness island with dedicated facilities for private group programmes, Kagi pairs the intimacy of an exclusive use buyout with a holistic curriculum covering fitness, nutrition, mindfulness and marine experiences. For leadership retreats and corporate wellness, it is without parallel.
