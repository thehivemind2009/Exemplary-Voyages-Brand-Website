Our Commitments

Designed to give back as much as it takes in

At Exemplary Voyages, how a journey is designed matters as much as where it goes. This page describes our commitments to the communities we work within, the environments we pass through, and the guests who trust us with their plans.

Our Approach

We treat sustainability as a selection criterion, not a footnote.

Every resort in our portfolio, every experience we recommend, and every community partner we work with is assessed before it appears in an Exemplary Voyages itinerary. We ask specific questions: who benefits from this booking, what environmental practices are in place, and what happens to the money after the guest leaves.

We also look inward. Our own team reflects the diversity we expect from the places we represent. Women hold senior positions across every department. Our staff come from different cultural, linguistic, and professional backgrounds, and we consider this range of perspective a direct contributor to the quality of the itineraries we design.

Six Areas of Practice

What our commitments look like in practice

Responsible Resort Curation

Each property we feature is assessed against environmental criteria before we recommend it. We prioritise resorts that have invested in coral restoration, renewable energy, responsible waste management, and locally sourced food. Prestige alone is not a reason to list a property. Where a resort's practices fall short of our standards, it does not appear in our collections.

Women in Leadership and Cultural Diversity

Women hold senior roles across operations, travel design, and client relations at Exemplary Voyages. This is not a policy adopted in response to external pressure: it reflects how the company was built. Our team includes people from different cultural and professional backgrounds, and we consider this range of perspective a direct contributor to the quality of the itineraries we design.

Sri Lankan Heritage in Focus

Sri Lanka's traditional industries are disappearing. Handloom weaving, Dumbara mat weaving, lacquerwork, artisan pottery, and traditional mask carving are all facing decline as younger generations move toward urban employment. We route guests toward these crafts deliberately, placing money directly with the families who carry this knowledge. This is among the most meaningful contributions a travel company can make.

Community Investment

We partner with community enterprises, cooperatives, and social enterprises across Sri Lanka, ensuring a meaningful portion of each itinerary's spend reaches local communities rather than being extracted by intermediaries. We do not work with operators whose pricing model separates guests from the people who create the experience. Our community partners are chosen for the directness of the link between a booking and a local benefit.

Youth Training and Skill Development

We offer structured internship and training programmes for young Sri Lankans entering the travel and hospitality industry. Participants spend time across client relations, itinerary design, and operations. We track their career progress after placement and consider the advancement of former interns an active part of our mission. We view this investment as a contribution to the long-term health of Sri Lankan tourism.

Regulatory Standards

As a company whose primary market is Europe, we hold ourselves to the standards that matter to European travellers and regulators. We comply with the EU Deforestation Regulation, ensuring none of the journeys we facilitate contribute to deforestation or forest degradation. We operate under full GDPR compliance: client data is collected with explicit consent, stored securely, and never shared with third parties without permission.

Regulatory Compliance

The two European standards we are fully committed to

We recognise that European guests approach travel with expectations shaped by domestic regulation and a well-developed awareness of greenwashing. We do not ask you to take our word for it. Below are the two regulatory frameworks that govern our operations, with a plain account of what compliance means for your journey.

If you have questions about how either framework applies to your booking, we are glad to answer them directly.

European Union
EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)

The EUDR requires that companies operating with the European market ensure their activities do not contribute to deforestation or forest degradation. We apply this by assessing each of our destination partners and experience providers against land use and sourcing criteria. Any operator whose supply chain cannot demonstrate deforestation-free practices is removed from our portfolio. This is an ongoing assessment, not a one-time check.

Data Protection
General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

We collect only the personal data required to design and deliver your journey. All data is stored securely, with access limited to team members who require it. We will never sell or share your information with third parties for commercial purposes. You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data at any time. Our full privacy policy is available on request by writing to inquiries@exemplaryvoyages.com.

Youth and Community

Investing in the people who make travel possible

The quality of a travel experience is inseparable from the wellbeing of the communities that create it. We invest in the next generation of Sri Lankan hospitality professionals and in the communities that sustain the natural and cultural environments our guests travel to see.

These are not peripheral activities. They are part of how Exemplary Voyages defines its role in the industry.

Internship Programme

Young Sri Lankans rotate across client relations, itinerary design, and operations during a structured placement, gaining a full view of how a boutique DMC works and leaving with skills recognised across the sector.

Career Tracking and Mentorship

We follow the professional progress of former interns and maintain an active relationship with our alumni. Mentorship and referral support continue beyond the placement period, and we measure our success partly by where these individuals go next.

Community Skill Development

We support skill development in the communities we partner with, with particular focus on traditional craft practitioners, guides, and local operators who benefit from exposure to an international client base and professional standards.
