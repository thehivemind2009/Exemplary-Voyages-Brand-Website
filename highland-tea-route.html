02
Day Two

Kandy: Batik & Angampora

After breakfast, enter one of Sri Lanka's most treasured traditional art forms through a Private Batik Design Session. Guided by a skilled and experienced artist, this hands on workshop offers a rare opportunity to learn the intricate process of batik making; from wax resist techniques to dyeing and final detailing. Each session runs approximately two and a half to three hours, giving you ample time to design and create your own personalised piece to carry home as a souvenir. Suitable for all skill levels, set in a relaxed and inspiring environment.

In the late afternoon, enter the world of Angampora; Sri Lanka's ancient martial art. Conducted by a highly experienced and traditionally trained instructor, this immersive session reveals the strength, discipline and grace behind one of the oldest and most revered martial art forms in South Asia. The 15:30 session weaves together history, philosophy and physical training in equal measure.

03
Day Three

Kandy to Ella

3.5 hours' drive

After breakfast, explore a working organic farm set in the Uva Highlands; a labour of love for its founders and local village staff. Hidden above Ravana Ella Falls, with views of Ella Rock and Lipton's Seat, the estate cultivates tea, cinnamon, coffee and lemongrass using entirely manual techniques. Walk the fields with your guide, visit the artisanal production centre and try your hand at plucking and rolling tea leaves. The experience culminates with a tasting of four signature hand made teas at a scenic point overlooking Ella Gap. A "Teaology" option is available for those who wish to pair each tea with dishes crafted to complement it. This experience is not available on Sundays or public holidays.

In the afternoon, discover Ella through its flavours on a curated food trail through handpicked local restaurants and bars; crispy papadam, frozen curd and an authentic rice and curry. The perfect way to taste the heart of the hill country, one bite at a time.

Day Four

Ella: Ravana Pool Club

After breakfast at the resort, make your way to Ravana Pool Club from 11am with VIP access to the pool area. Set amid Ella's rolling green hills, the club offers a lively yet scenic place to unwind. Relax beside the infinity pool, ease into the heated Jacuzzi or settle into a day bed with sweeping views towards Ella Rock and Little Adam's Peak. Relaxed dining and a vibrant poolside atmosphere bring a contemporary touch to Sri Lanka's hill country.

The great swing and zip liner are also available for those who prefer the hills with a little more speed. The evening is yours entirely.

Day Five

Handunugoda & Talpe

4 hours' drive

After breakfast, check out and travel south. Hidden within Sri Lanka's lush southern countryside, Handunugoda is a family owned low country estate celebrated for its rare Virgin White Tea. Inspired by an ancient Chinese tradition, the delicate tea buds are harvested and processed without being touched by bare hands, preserving their subtle and distinctive character.

Walk through the plantation, explore the working tea factory and discover the craftsmanship behind this singular tea, concluding with a guided tasting. It is a fascinating introduction to a lesser known expression of Ceylon tea, just a short journey from the southern coast.

Check in at Villa Selenya in Talpe, where the evening is yours to settle into the villa at your own pace.

Talpe & Galle
06
Day Six

Cinnamon Island & Galle Fort

After breakfast, board a boat for a serene ride to Cinnamon Island; an immersive encounter with the only family that lives there, who have preserved the traditional art of hand rolling cinnamon sticks for generations. Gliding through tranquil waters and lush greenery, you arrive to be welcomed into their aromatic world. In a mud hut surrounded by bundles of cinnamon bark, witness the meticulous process of hand rolling each stick with precision and care; a tradition passed down through generations. The family concludes the experience with a delightful local lunch.

In the evening, walk the ramparts of the Galle Fort; one of the best preserved colonial sea fortifications in Asia. Explore its lanes of Dutch colonial architecture, browse local boutiques and watch the sun descend behind the lighthouse. A gentle, unhurried way to end the day.

Day Seven

Talpe to Colombo

2.5 hours' drive

After breakfast, proceed to Colombo and into the inner world of Ceylon tea with a professional tasting session led by experts who regularly attend the Sri Lankan tea auction. Visit one of the country's oldest producers for exclusive access to the full production process; refining, blending, packaging and the unique grading system that has shaped Ceylon tea's global reputation.

Learn the subtle differences in grain, texture, colour, strength and flavour across high, mid and low grown teas, and understand how master blenders craft each batch to meet the distinct preferences of tea markets around the world.

Alternative Evening

Explore Colombo's busy streets after dark; a different city entirely when the heat drops and the restaurants fill. End with some last minute shopping before a final night at the Cinnamon Life.

Colombo to Airport: A Final Morning

After a final leisurely breakfast in Colombo, check out and begin your private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport along the coastal expressway, timed precisely to your flight. Assistance with your luggage on arrival, a quiet farewell, and then Sri Lanka recedes behind you; taking with you the memories, stories and quiet moments that made this journey entirely your own.
