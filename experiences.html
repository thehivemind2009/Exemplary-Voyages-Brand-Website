Sri Lanka

Curated Experiences

Beyond the itinerary, Sri Lanka reveals itself in the moments between. Fifteen curated encounters with its culture, craft, cuisine and wilderness, added to any journey on request.

How It Works

Each experience is designed to deepen your
connection to the island.

Select any experience below and we will weave it seamlessly into your itinerary. Every experience is private, led by a specialist, and arranged around your travel schedule. To enquire about an experience or to add one to a planned journey, simply click Book Now and we will be in touch.

Curated Experiences
Mirissa
Mirissa · South Coast
Nov – Apr 7 Hours Departs 6:30 am

Private Cruise for Whale & Dolphin Watching

Sail into deep waters beyond Mirissa aboard a private yacht in search of spinner dolphins, bottlenose dolphins, blue whales, sperm whales and pilot whales. The boat may pause in calmer waters for swimming or paddleboarding. Breakfast and lunch are served on board.

Ella
Ella · Hill Country
All Year 3 Hours Departs 4:00 pm

Ella Food Trail

Explore Ella through its flavors on this curated trail visiting handpicked local restaurants and bars. Journey through crispy papadam, a frozen curd experience, and authentic rice and curry. The perfect way to taste the heart of the hill country, one bite at a time.

Book Now →
Ella
Ella · Hill Country
All Year 3 Hours Departs 9:00 am

Estate Tour & Tea

Explore a working organic farm in Ella and understand the manual techniques behind producing tea, coffee, cinnamon and preserves. Stroll through fields of tea, cinnamon and lemongrass, try your hand at plucking and rolling tea leaves, then end with a tasting of four signature handmade teas.

Book Now →
Kandy
Kandy · Central Province
All Year 2 Hours Departs 8:30 am

The World of Batik

Guided by a skilled Batik artist, this immersive workshop takes you through one of Sri Lanka's most treasured art forms: wax resist techniques, dyeing and final detailing. Suitable for all skill levels, the session is as meditative as it is creative.

Book Now →
Kandy
Kandy · Central Province
All Year 2 Hours Departs 11:00 am

The Three Temple Loop

Discover three remarkable temples, each carrying a different story. Begin at Gadaladeniya, step back to the 12th century. A tuk tuk ride through paddy fields leads to Lankatilaka, then to Embekka, home to a Hindu shrine unlike any other, with views that seem to stretch without end.

Book Now →
Kandy
Kandy · Central Province
All Year 2 Hours 8:30 am or 3:30 pm

Angampora: Sri Lankan Martial Art

Enter the world of Angampora, Sri Lanka's ancient martial art. Conducted by a traditionally trained instructor, this rare experience blends history, philosophy and physical training, offering an intimate understanding of one of South Asia's oldest and most revered art forms.

Book Now →
Kandy
Kandy · Central Province
All Year 3 Hours 8:30 am or 4:00 pm

The Laksha Legacy

Immerse yourself in the ancient art of lacquer crafting, where natural resins are applied to wood in vibrant, glossy layers. You will witness the meticulous layering process and engage in crafting your own lacquer piece, a beautiful keepsake from the heart of Kandy.

Book Now →
Tangalle
Tangalle · South Coast
All Year 2 Hours 8:00 am or 3:00 pm

The Art of Pottery

Discover the timeless art of traditional pottery with a family in Tangalle who have honed this craft for generations. Feel the clay between your fingers, learn the techniques passed down through time, and leave with a beautiful pottery piece of your own making as a keepsake.

Book Now →
Galle
Galle · South Coast
All Year 1 Hour Departs 9:00 am

Ceylon Lace Making

An intimate beeralu lace making workshop held at the local home of your host in Galle. From drawing patterns on graph paper to winding threads around wooden bobbins and weaving around pins, you will take home a small piece of handmade lace as a souvenir.

Book Now →
Polonnaruwa
Polonnaruwa · Cultural Triangle
All Year 3.5 Hours Departs 8:30 am

Polonnaruwa by Cycle

Explore the UNESCO World Heritage ancient city of Polonnaruwa by bicycle. An unforgettable journey back to the golden era of Sri Lanka's Sinhalese kingdom. Pedal through some of the most impressive ruins and monuments on the island at your own pace, guided through history.

Book Now →
Ritigala
Ritigala · Cultural Triangle
All Year 2 Hours Departs 8:30 am

Discover Ritigala

Explore Ritigala's jungle enshrouded paths on a guided walk and hear the secrets of the forest dwelling Buddhist monks who were once its inhabitants. As you ascend through ancient jungle over untouched steps, it is hard not to feel like you are discovering Ritigala for the very first time.

Book Now →
Colombo
Colombo · Western Province
All Year 3 Hours Departs 9:00 am

Bawa in Colombo

A guided architectural journey through Colombo tracing the legacy of Sri Lanka's great architect Geoffrey Bawa. Begin at Seema Malaka Temple, continue to Number 11, the hub of Geoffrey's city life, and finish at Gallery Café, appreciating another of Bawa's triumphs over a drink.

Book Now →
Galle
Galle · South Coast
All Year 2 Hours Departs 9:00 am

Elephant Mask Painting

A delightful creative activity led by skilled local artists, designed for younger travellers but enjoyed by all ages. Discover the world of elephants through painting, combining art and Sri Lankan culture in an immersive session that ends with vibrant elephant masks to take home.

Book Now →
Negombo
Negombo · Western Province
All Year 4 Hours Departs 8:00 am

Lagoon Crab Experience

Begin at the dock, board a boat into the Negombo lagoon and observe the traditional crab catching process from the water. The adventure culminates with a generous seafood lunch, fresh, local and entirely memorable. A wonderful introduction to coastal Sri Lankan life.

Book Now →
Kitulgala
Kitulgala · Sabaragamuwa
All Year 3 Hours Departs 8:30 am

Rafting & Canoeing

After a safety briefing, jump right into action on a 6.5km stretch of river. A combined rafting and canyoning option takes you down the rapids before heading up the Kelani River tributary for canyoning, including a few natural waterslides along the way.

Book Now →
