08 Properties chosen for design, reef access, and privacy
01
Soneva Jani

Glass-floored villas with retractable roofs that open to the night sky. A waterslide into the lagoon and a thriving house reef below. Barefoot luxury at its most celebrated, set in the pristine Noonu Atoll.

Book Now
02
Patina Maldives

Contemporary design meets ecological conscience across the Fari Islands. Overwater pool villas with direct lagoon access and a resident arts district. Beauty and purpose together, unlike anything else in the archipelago.

Book Now
03
Soneva Fushi

The original barefoot luxury, with villas above the water and a house reef ranking among the finest in the Maldives. The resort that defined the category. It still holds that ground today.

Book Now
04
Ritz Carlton Maldives

Legendary service brought to an overwater setting of striking contemporary design in Fari Islands. Private pools, open-air pavilions, and a lagoon that glows in afternoon light. The Ritz Carlton standard, without compromise.

Book Now
05
Ozen Reserve Bolifushi

A fully all-inclusive overwater experience with gourmet dining, unlimited water sports, and a spa, all included. Overwater pool villas just 35 minutes from Male by speedboat. Nothing withheld, nothing left to arrange.

Book Now
06
Gili Lankanfushi

Pioneers of the overwater villa concept, with sprawling two-storey residences above a turquoise lagoon. Every villa has a dedicated Mr Friday personal butler. The overwater experience at its most generous and unhurried.

Book Now
07
St. Regis Maldives

Inspired by the manta ray, Vommuli Island delivers overwater villas with butler service and interiors that read as art. A rooftop observatory, exceptional dining, and the St. Regis standard throughout.

Book Now
08
Rah Gili

An adults-only retreat within a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve in Raa Atoll. Overwater villas above a protected lagoon with snorkelling and diving of exceptional quality. Remote, quiet, and deeply restorative.
