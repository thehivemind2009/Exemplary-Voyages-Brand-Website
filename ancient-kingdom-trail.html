Day One

Arrival & Negombo

30 min from Airport

Your journey begins the moment you land. A private transfer from Bandaranaike International Airport carries you the short distance to Negombo, a coastal town of fishing villages, Dutch canals and unhurried warmth. This first evening asks nothing of you but rest.

Let the sea air and the sound of the Indian Ocean set the tone for what lies ahead.

Anuradhapura
02
Day Two

Anuradhapura & Mihintale

4 hours' drive

The drive north carries you into another era entirely. Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka's first and greatest ancient capital, is a sacred landscape of monumental stupas, serene reservoirs and centuries-old ruins; where the island's history still feels profoundly alive. Explore at your own pace through the early afternoon, guided by your private historian.

As the day softens, continue to Mihintale; the birthplace of Buddhism in Sri Lanka. Climb through ancient shrines and forested pathways to sweeping views filled with spirituality and stillness. This is the hill where, in the third century BC, a prince delivered the teachings that would shape the nation.

03
Day Three

Polonnaruwa & Sigiriya

3 hours' drive

After breakfast, proceed to Polonnaruwa; Sri Lanka's second ancient kingdom, which reveals a remarkable era of royal ambition, spiritual devotion and sophisticated engineering. Explore the remains of grand palaces, sacred temples and beautifully carved stone statues, set beside the vast Parakrama Samudraya. This timeless landscape, where the splendour of a medieval capital still endures, is best discovered by bicycle: a slower pace that lets the grandeur settle.

By late afternoon, arrive at Sigiriya, where the extraordinary rock fortress rises above the surrounding forest and sets the scene for tomorrow.

04
Day Four

Sigiriya & Minneriya

Rising dramatically above Sri Lanka's Cultural Triangle, Sigiriya is an extraordinary fifth century royal citadel and UNESCO World Heritage Site. Begin the ascent early, before the sun reaches its full strength. Pass the ancient water gardens, marvel at the vivid frescoes, and push through the monumental Lion's Gate to reach palace ruins and sweeping views from the summit; a remarkable testament to the island's artistry, engineering and royal ambition.

As the afternoon heat softens, venture into Minneriya National Park for a game drive across open grasslands, forest and the shores of its ancient reservoir. Watch for elephants emerging in the golden evening light, alongside deer, monkeys and abundant birdlife. During the dry season, large herds may gather near the water; creating one of Sri Lanka's most memorable wildlife spectacles.

Day Five

Kandy

2.5 hours' drive

Descend from the Cultural Triangle into the hill country. Kandy, the last kingdom of Sri Lanka, is the island's cultural and spiritual heart. Surrounded by forested hills and centred around its tranquil lake, this former royal capital carries its history with quiet dignity. The revered Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic stands at its centre; home to the tooth relic of the Buddha and a place of continuous devotion for over fifteen centuries.

Visit the temple in the late afternoon, when the air is cooler and the rituals feel most intimate. The evening is entirely your own.

06
Day Six

Kandy: The Three Temple Loop

Today belongs to the villages, temples and traditions that exist just beyond the city. Begin at Gadaladeniya and step into the twelfth century; a temple where Buddhism and Hinduism have quietly influenced one another for generations. Notice the grandeur of the architecture, the presence of the Buddha statue and the remarkable artistry of every carved surface.

A tuk tuk carries you through paddy fields to Lankatilaka, where your guide traces the foundations of Buddhism in Sri Lanka and the role it continues to play for three quarters of the population. Before the third temple, pause at a working plantation; coffee, tea and pepper growing side by side in the same red earth. Finally, Embekka, with its Hindu shrine unlike any other, and a view of the surrounding hills that seems to extend without end.

Day Seven

Nuwara Eliya

2.5 hours' drive

The road climbs into cooler air and a different world. Set high in Sri Lanka's mist-covered central hills, Nuwara Eliya is a graceful retreat of emerald tea estates, cascading waterfalls and mountain calm. Its colonial architecture, manicured gardens and serene lakes lend the town a timeless character; while the surrounding plantations reveal the heritage and craft behind world-renowned Ceylon tea.

Visit a tea estate in the afternoon; walking the rows, watching the sorting and drying process, and tasting the leaves at their finest. A waterfall or two on the way, then the evening at leisure in the cool highland air.

08
Day Eight

Nuwara Eliya: The High Plains

Begin the morning at Moon Plains; a peaceful highland landscape of rolling grasslands, vegetable farms and distant mountain ranges. A short scenic drive leads to the viewpoint known as Mini World's End, where clear weather reveals sweeping views across Sri Lanka's central hills. It is a place to simply stand and take in the scale of the country you have been travelling through.

Alternative Experience

Horton Plains National Park, a UNESCO listed wilderness of cloud forest, open grassland and endemic wildlife. Follow the scenic walking trail to World's End and Baker's Falls, ideally beginning early before the morning mist settles over the plateau.

09
Day Nine

Weligama

5.5 hours' drive

The longest drive of the journey, but a beautiful one. The road descends through the hill country and eventually reaches the southern coast, where a very different Sri Lanka awaits. Weligama curves around a broad, sheltered bay of golden sand and gentle surf. Its unhurried rhythm and celebrated coastal restaurants invite slow evenings beside the Indian Ocean.

Tonight is not for sightseeing. It is for the sound of waves, a long dinner, and the knowledge that this extraordinary journey is nearly complete.

Weligama to Airport: A Final Morning

After a final leisurely breakfast beside the southern coast, your private transfer departs for Bandaranaike International Airport along the coastal expressway; timed precisely to your flight. Assistance with luggage on arrival, a quiet farewell, and then Sri Lanka recedes in the rear window; taking with you the memories, stories and quiet moments that made this journey entirely your own.
